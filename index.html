Leadership & Vision

“A Pilates studio is more than just a place to exercise, it is a spatial sanctuary for mental clarity.”

Holding full certification from STOTT PILATES, one of the world’s leading Pilates education and certification institutions, and bringing experience in designing wellness sanctuaries across Indonesia, Anastasia May founded Harmony Studio & Harmony Project in 2024.

The primary focus is to integrate the principles of movement ergonomics with prehabilitation and rehabilitation to prevent and address injuries. Every Reformer layout, indirect lighting scheme, and material selection is carefully considered to support the intensity of Pilates practice while minimizing visual distractions.
